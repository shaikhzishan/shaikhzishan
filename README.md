## Selected Projects

### FMCG Business Intelligence & Customer Analytics

**Python · Business Intelligence · Customer Analytics · Statistical Analysis**

An end-to-end FMCG-style business intelligence case study using the Olist Brazilian e-commerce dataset. The project analyzes revenue, customers, product categories, sellers, delivery performance, customer reviews, and forecasting.

**Highlights**

* Analyzed **99,441 orders**, 96,096 customers, 3,095 sellers, and 71 product categories.
* Identified approximately **R$15.74M** in revenue/payment value across valid non-cancelled orders.
* Found that approximately **97% of customers placed only one order**, highlighting a major retention opportunity.
* Examined the relationship between delivery performance and customer satisfaction, finding a correlation of **r = -0.336** with **p < 0.001**.
* Found that **557 of 3,053 sellers generated approximately 80% of revenue**, highlighting seller concentration.
* Compared forecasting approaches and found a **seasonal-naive baseline (43.0% MAPE)** outperformed Prophet (88.1%) in the tested setup.

**Tools:** `Python` `Pandas` `NumPy` `SciPy` `Statsmodels` `Prophet` `Scikit-learn` `Plotly`

### Multi-Asset Portfolio Optimization

**Python · Financial Analytics · Quantitative Research · Risk Analysis**

A quantitative research project testing whether portfolio optimization that performs well historically continues to generalize to unseen data.

The study evaluates **64 securities** across equities, ETFs, bonds, commodities, and crypto, comparing Equal Weight, Minimum Variance, and Maximum Sharpe portfolio construction strategies.

**Highlights**

* Used chronological **70/30 out-of-sample testing** and six rolling walk-forward windows.
* Compared return, Sharpe ratio, drawdown, turnover, and performance degradation.
* Maximum Sharpe achieved the strongest full-sample Sharpe ratio of **2.40**, but Equal Weight achieved the strongest out-of-sample generalization.
* Equal Weight achieved **20.05% out-of-sample return**, **1.18 OOS Sharpe**, and a **98.46 Generalization Score** in the reported experiment.
* Used statistical testing to avoid treating observed performance differences as automatically meaningful.

**Tools:** `Python` `Pandas` `NumPy` `Portfolio Optimization` `Statistical Testing` `Data Visualization`

[View the project](https://github.com/shaikhzishan/multi-asset-portfolio-optimization)
